01 ABOUT RAMMIYAM

MORE THAN A HOME

We build places where life happens.

Rammiyam Homes is a premier residential construction company founded in 1999 by Shri. M.S. Elangeshwaran, Shri. M.S. Maheshwaran, and Shri. R. Saravanan. Headquartered in Chennai, the company has grown steadily over two decades to become one of the most trusted names in residential real estate development, specialising in high-quality apartment projects across the Velachery and Madipakkam corridors.

Since inception, Rammiyam Homes has successfully completed more than 165+ residential apartment projects in and around Chennai, with 7 projects currently under active construction. Two further landmark projects are in the pipeline to fuel the company's continued growth and expansion. The firm has proudly endowed over 1000+ families with their dream homes, cementing its reputation as a builder of lasting relationships — not just buildings.
